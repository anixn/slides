About This Project

🌾 Early Warning Systems & Disaster Readiness Framework

Project: Developing Early Warning Systems and Disaster Readiness Framework through Resilient Community Engagement

Funding: 2nd Call for ICSSR Longitudinal Studies in Social and Human Sciences

Team: Dr. Anurag Srivastava, Dr. Prateek Kumar Singh, Dr. Ankit Deshmukh, Dr. Shreekant Varshney, Sandeep Panday

Focus Areas

  • Urban Flood: Gujarat districts facing increased flood frequency and swapping trends
  • Agricultural Drought: Saurashtra and Kutch regions with depleted reservoirs
  • Survey: 10,000 respondents (3,000 Step 1 + 7,000 Step 2) across 34 Gujarat districts

Dashboard Tools

Built with Quarto, Leaflet, Plotly, Highcharter, and DT. Theme: Material-inspired high-contrast on white.

Boundary data: udit-001/india-maps-data (GitHub). Hazard indicators are illustrative placeholders pending field data collection.
